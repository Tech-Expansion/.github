# TEX Labs

We build software for businesses and keep it running after launch: web apps, backend systems, cloud setup and AI
features. Bring us an idea or an existing product; we take outsourced projects from the first version to everyday use.

## What we do

- **Build your product**: a web app your customers can sign up for, pay for and use, with admin tools for your team
- **Make it hold up**: APIs and background services designed to stay fast and reliable as usage grows
- **Run it in the cloud**: setup on AWS or Azure, automatic releases, monitoring and alerts, so issues are caught early
- **Connect your systems**: payment providers, third-party services and the tools you already use
- **Add AI where it pays off**: automate repetitive work and improve the experience for your users
- **Use your data**: live dashboards, search and reports

Built with proven tools: TypeScript and Node.js, .NET, React, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, AWS and Azure.

## Why work with us

- **We run our own products.** Our team built and operates the products below, so we know what it takes to keep
  software working after launch.
- **Quality is built in.** Changes are reviewed before release, releases are automated, and live systems are monitored
  with alerts.
- **You work with senior engineers directly**, with an engineering manager who owns the delivery.
- **Backed by about $200K in grant funding** for our product work in 2024.

## Our products

- [Midnight Explorer](https://www.midnightexplorer.com): real-time search and analytics over a public blockchain
- [Midnight Explorer API](https://api-management.midnightexplorer.com/): a SaaS API platform with API keys, rate
  limits, usage analytics and subscription billing
- [CKBolt](https://ckbolt.texlabs.org/): a payment and swap hub, testnet prototype
